VECTORFALL

A wireframe space trading and combat game in the spirit of the 1984 classic. Buy low in farming systems, sell high in industrial ones, and keep your ship in one piece between the two.

Difficulty

Flight controls

↑↓ pitch   ←→ or AD roll   WS throttle   Space fire laser
C dock when the slot is lined up, or engage the docking computer   T fire a missile at the ship in your sights
E ECM   B energy bomb   Shift hold to boost (burns fuel)   G galactic hyperdrive
M galactic chart   J hyperspace to the selected system

Line up the station's docking slot in the middle of your screen, slow down, and press C. Pirates pay a bounty and sometimes drop a cargo canister; asteroids give ore if you have room in the hold. On a phone, drag on the left of the screen to steer.

Docked

Tap a system to select it.

Copy the code to back up or move your commander; paste a code and import to restore it. Save files do the same through a file on disk, and carry the active universe with them when it is not the default.

A universe file defines the goods, player ships, galaxies, wireframe models, name syllables and colours. Build one in the universe editor, load it here, and it applies to every commander on this device.
